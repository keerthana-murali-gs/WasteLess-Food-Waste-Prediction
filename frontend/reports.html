Actionable Kitchen Waste Reduction Strategies
Weekend Surge Calibration

Dinner items like Chicken Biryani and Tandoori Roti observe a 20-25% surge on Fridays and Saturdays. Use the ML predictor's weekend feature rather than static daily prep targets.

Perishable Item Batching

Paneer Butter Masala and Dal Makhani account for a disproportionate share of financial losses. Prepare smaller mid-day batches and replenish on-demand during dinner shifts.

Safety Buffer Calibration

Maintain an 8% safety buffer for high-demand breakfast items (Dosa, Idli) to prevent stockouts while keeping discarded waste safely below the 4% target threshold.
